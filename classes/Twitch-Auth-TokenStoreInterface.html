TokenStoreInterface
in
  • Application

TokenStoreInterface.php : 24

Somewhere durable to keep the OAuth token pair.

Twitch rotates the refresh token on every refresh and invalidates the old one, so a client that refreshes without persisting the result works until the process exits and then locks itself out. Twitch writes through to a store on every token change when one is configured.
