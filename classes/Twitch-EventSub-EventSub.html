EventSub.php : 42

The EventSub WebSocket transport (`wss://eventsub.wss.twitch.tv/ws`).

Handles session_welcome (captures the session id), session_keepalive, session_reconnect (transparent hand-off to the new URL), revocation, and notification. A notification fires two events on the Twitch client: eventsub with [type, event, metadata], and eventsub.<type> with [event, metadata] (e.g. eventsub.channel.chat.message).

subscribe() registers a subscription against the current session.
